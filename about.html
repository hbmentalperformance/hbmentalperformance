About

Hi, I'm Hanna.

Professional soccer player, athlete mentor, and founder of HB Mental Performance.

My Story

Why mental performance

Playing professional soccer taught me that talent and physical preparation only get an athlete so far. What separates good from great, and great from consistent, is the mental game — how you handle pressure, setbacks, and the noise of comparison.

My own path took me from playing Division I college soccer to professional contracts across Europe — Iceland, Hungary, Turkey, and Italy, including a Champions League run along the way. Every move meant starting over in a new country, a new locker room, sometimes a new language — proving myself again with none of the support system I'd had growing up. That's where the mental game stopped being an abstract idea and became the thing that actually got me through.

That's what led me to pursue sport psychology and start mentoring athletes directly, and eventually to building HB Mental Performance — a practice dedicated to helping athletes train their mind with the same intention they train their body.

Philosophy

You vs. You

Every athlete I work with is chasing something different — a starting spot, a scholarship, a pro contract, a comeback. But the work is always the same: get out of your own way, and compete against who you were yesterday.
