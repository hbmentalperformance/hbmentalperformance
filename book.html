You vs You: Win From Within

I started chasing this dream before I even understood what it would take. You vs You: Win From Within pulls back the curtain on all of it — the injuries that almost ended things before they started, a recruiting process that felt like an audition for my whole future, and years spent overseas proving myself in new countries, new locker rooms, always a new language, as a woman finding my place in the sport. It's about the mindset that got me through, some advice on nutrition along the way, and the one idea that kept showing up no matter what: being the best you you can be.
